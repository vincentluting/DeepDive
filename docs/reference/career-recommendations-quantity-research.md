# Career Recommendations: Optimal Quantity Research

> Product decision research for Deep Dive's 3+2 architecture.
> Compiled 2026-03-17 from 25+ academic sources and industry benchmarks.

## Decision: 3 Primary + 2 Exploratory Directions

### Cognitive Psychology Evidence

| Source | Finding |
|--------|---------|
| **Iyengar & Lepper (2000)** "Jam Study" | 6 options → 30% purchase rate; 24 options → 3% purchase rate (10x difference). Satisfaction also higher with fewer options. |
| **Schwartz** "Paradox of Choice" | More options → maximizer behavior → higher salary but lower satisfaction & higher depression. 3 deep recommendations nudge satisficing ("good enough" strategy), producing better subjective outcomes. |
| **Cowan (2001)** Working Memory | Effective capacity for complex information is **3-4 chunks**, not Miller's 7±2. Career directions are multi-attribute complex concepts. |
| **Chernev et al. (2015)** Meta-analysis (99 studies, N=7,202) | Career decisions hit all 4 choice overload moderators at high levels: complexity, task difficulty, preference uncertainty, accuracy goals. Fewer options strongly recommended. |
| **Reutskaja et al. (2022)** Cross-national study | Inverted U-shape between choice set size and satisfaction. Sweet spot well below large-assortment end. Too few = choice deprivation; too many = overload. |

### Career Counseling Theory

| Theorist | Framework | Recommendation |
|----------|-----------|---------------|
| **Gati** PIC Model (2001) | Prescreening → In-depth Exploration → Choice | Prescreening: ≤7; In-depth exploration: **3-4** |
| **Gati (2022)** List quality study (20 counselors + 103 grad students) | "Identity exploration" context (our target) = midlife re-training analog | **Fewer than 7, likely 3-5** |
| **Savickas** Career Construction Theory | Narrative meaning-making > exhaustive comparison | Fewer, deeply contextualized "stories to try on" |
| **Super** Career Development Theory | Crystallization → Specification → Implementation | Progressive narrowing: broad → focused |
| **Stumpf et al. (1983)** Career Exploration Survey | Focused exploration > diffuse exploration for decidedness | **Depth > breadth** |

### Cross-Cultural Dimension (Highly Relevant for Deep Dive)

- Collectivist-influenced users benefit from **fewer, deeply explained options** suitable for family discussion
- 5+ broad options feel overwhelming and culturally inappropriate
- 3 primary directions = discussable, non-paralyzing number
- Sources: Leong & Lowe (2005), UNT Family Influence on Career Development study

### The 3+2 Architecture Rationale

```
┌─────────────────────────────────────────┐
│  3 Primary Directions                    │
│  — Deep narrative: why it fits, match    │
│    rationale, growth path, challenges    │
│  — For serious exploration & discussion  │
├─────────────────────────────────────────┤
│  2 Exploratory Directions               │
│  — Light treatment: "you might not have  │
│    considered this, but..."              │
│  — Satisfies curiosity, prevents choice  │
│    deprivation (Reutskaja's inverted U)  │
└─────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

- **3 primary** = Cowan working memory limit, Schwartz satisficing nudge, Savickas narrative construction
- **+2 exploratory** = Prevents Reutskaja's "choice deprivation" (left side of inverted U), maintains exploration feel
- **Total 5** = Gallup CliftonStrengths validated "Top 5" psychological anchor
